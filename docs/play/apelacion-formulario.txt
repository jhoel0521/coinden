The financial features declaration was rejected as inaccurate. I changed it from "no financial features" to "Other", with this description: CoinDen is a personal expense notebook. People type, dictate or photograph what they have ALREADY paid, and the app only adds and subtracts those notes. It does NOT move money, make payments or transfers, hold funds or crypto, connect to banks or exchanges, or offer loans, credit, investments or financial advice. A "wallet" is only a label the user types ("Cash", "My bank") with a balance entered by hand; it is linked to no real account. I did not select "mobile payments and digital wallets" or "cryptocurrency wallet" because the app does neither. There are no accounts or servers, and data is encrypted on the phone. Source code: https://github.com/jhoel0521/coinden . If the Finance category causes the confusion, I can move the listing to Productivity.
