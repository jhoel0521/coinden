Privacy policy

Effective October 30, 2026

Summary

CoinDen is a spending notebook: it records what you already spent and does not move money. There is no account, no CoinDen servers, no ads and no analytics of our own. What you record is stored encrypted only on your phone, is never sent anywhere, and you can erase all of it whenever you want from the app.

What data the app keeps

Your wallets (the name you give them, their type, currency and balance), your movements, drafts, categories, rates and notes, and the text you dictate or type.

The photos, PDFs and audio of receipts you take or share with CoinDen. A receipt may include account numbers, names and transaction numbers. CoinDen keeps it as received so you can review it later, and never sends it anywhere.

Your preferences (language, lock and Google reader) and a randomly generated identifier for this installation, which never leaves the phone either.

Where it lives and how it is protected

Only on your phone, in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read.

The whole database is encrypted with SQLCipher (AES-256). Its key is a random value that is itself encrypted with a key from Android's secure store (Android Keystore), which cannot be taken off the phone.

Photos, PDFs and audio are stored in separate files, each encrypted with AES-256-GCM using another key from the secure store, different from the database key. The temporary files needed to read a PDF or record audio are deleted when done.

You can protect the app with your phone's fingerprint or PIN. With the lock on, neither screenshots nor the recent apps view show your balances.

No copies in the cloud

The app turns off Android backup: Android does not upload CoinDen's data to your Google account, and CoinDen has no backup of its own. If Android moves your apps to a new phone and copies CoinDen files, they arrive encrypted and cannot be opened, because the key stays in the old phone's secure store.

What can leave the phone

Only this, and always because you ask for it. Your movements, photos, text and amounts are never sent.

Voice models: if you download them, the app requests them from Hugging Face (huggingface.co). It is a public download: nothing of yours is sent, although, as with any website visit, that server sees your IP address.

Language packs and receipt templates: downloaded only when you ask, from CoinDen's site (jhoel0521.github.io, on GitHub Pages). They are public data and nothing of yours is sent.

Public exchange rates: today the app does not query any. When you can set up a rate source, the app will request only that public rate, without sending anything of yours.

Links: the site, the repository and the licenses you open from Profile open in your browser.

Google receipt reader

It is optional and off by default. If you turn it on, hard-to-read photos are also read with Google's ML Kit, on the phone. Google only receives technical usage metrics: phone model and system, app version, an identifier for this installation, timings, sizes, events and errors. It never receives your photos, text or amounts. Without it, the app reads with a free reader that sends nothing. You can turn it off at any time in Profile → Privacy. The full list of what it receives is in the data ML Kit collects, published by Google, and the rest in its terms.

Permissions

Camera: only when you tap Photo, to take a picture of a receipt.

Microphone: only while you dictate or record context. Speech is turned into text on the phone.

Internet: only for the downloads you ask for and, if you turn it on, the Google reader.

Fingerprint or PIN lock: checked by your phone's system. CoinDen never receives or stores your fingerprint or PIN.

How to erase your data, and your rights

In the app: Profile → Erase my data → Erase everything. If the lock is on, it asks for your fingerprint or PIN. The database, its key, the photos, PDFs and audio, the processing queue and your preferences are removed from this phone, and the app returns to the welcome screen as if freshly installed. Downloaded voice models and packs are kept, because they are public data. Only the phone where you confirm is erased.

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage from Android settings, also removes all its data.

Since CoinDen does not hold your data on any server, there is no copy that must also be erased and no data we could hand over. You exercise access, correction and erasure yourself on your phone: you see and correct your movements in the app, and erase everything from Profile.

What we never do

We do not sell, rent or share data. We do not show ads. There are no trackers.

Future changes

If CoinDen adds features that use the network in the future (for example, public exchange rates or an encrypted backup to your own cloud), they will be optional, explained in the app before they are turned on, and this policy will be updated first.

Children

CoinDen is not directed at children under 13.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, open an issue in CoinDen's repository.
