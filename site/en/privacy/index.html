Privacy policy

Effective October 26, 2026

Summary

CoinDen does not collect your data. There is no account, no CoinDen servers, no ads and no analytics of its own. What you record is stored encrypted on your phone and is not sent anywhere.

What data the app handles and where it lives

Your wallets, movements, drafts, categories, rates and notes, and the photos, PDFs and audio of your receipts, are stored only on your phone, in an encrypted database (AES-256) whose key is protected by Android's secure storage.

The app makes no automatic cloud backups. If you clear the app's data or uninstall it, everything is deleted.

Permissions

Camera: only when you tap Photo, to photograph a receipt.

Microphone: only while you dictate. Speech is turned into text on the phone.

Internet: only to download voice models when you ask for them.

Fingerprint or PIN lock: checked by your phone's system. CoinDen never receives or stores your fingerprint or PIN.

What can leave the phone

Voice models: if you download them, the app requests them from Hugging Face (huggingface.co). It is a public download: nothing of yours is sent, although, as with any website visit, that server sees your IP address.

Enhanced Google reader (optional, off by default): if you turn it on, photos are also read with Google's ML Kit on the phone, and Google receives only technical usage data (phone model and system, app version, an identifier for this installation, timings, sizes, events and errors). It never receives your photos, texts or amounts. You can turn it off at any time in Profile → Privacy. You can see the full list of what it receives in the data ML Kit collects, published by Google, and the rest in its terms.

What you share with CoinDen from other apps is stored on your phone, like everything else.

What we never do

We do not sell, rent or share data. We show no ads. There are no trackers.

Future changes

If CoinDen adds features that use the network (public exchange rates or an encrypted backup to your own cloud), they will be optional, explained in the app before they are turned on, and this policy will be updated first.

Children

CoinDen is not directed at children under 13.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, open an issue in the CoinDen repository.
